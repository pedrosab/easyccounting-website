Who we are

Built by someone who's sat on the other side of the numbers.

Easyccounting was started by a finance executive with senior-level experience running finance at the CFO level — corporate finance, financial planning, month-end close, the works. That background is why this exists.

Why this exists

Small businesses deserve the same discipline as big ones.

Larger companies have finance teams that keep their numbers accurate, reconciled, and ready to act on. Most small businesses don't have that — either they do it themselves, in the gaps between everything else, or they hand it off and hope for the best. Easyccounting exists to bring that same level of discipline to small businesses, without the overhead of hiring a full finance team.

Our approach

Institutional-grade process, small-business price.

The systems and habits that keep a company's books clean at any size are the same ones — consistent categorization, real reconciliation, a close process that's actually followed every month. We built Easyccounting to run that process efficiently, so it's accessible at a flat $145/month instead of the cost of hiring in-house.
